# Planned work presented as content with deliveries

The library and calendar read planned work as content grouped with its deliveries, not as the legacy Instagram-shaped post, so a creative appears once and each destination reports for itself. A delivery's situation is derived in one platform-neutral place and handed to the views as a tone, a label, and the detail behind it, which keeps the states that must never be conflated apart at the surface too: a retryable failure says it is retrying, a credential problem asks for that one connection to be reconnected, and an uncertain outcome names the platform to check and says retries are paused, rather than reading as a failure the app will quietly try again. Content belongs wherever its destinations put it — the same creative can be a draft in one place and already scheduled in another, and content with no destination yet is still a draft the creator can finish — so a status is never invented for the whole item. The calendar plots deliveries rather than content, because a delivery is what owns a scheduled time; published work continues to come from the platform's own record through its adapter, has no local delivery, and shows only the figures the platform actually reports. Deleting planned work removes the content and its deliveries together, refused durably while any destination holds a publishing claim, so the record the scheduler reads and the record the creator sees cannot drift apart. The legacy Post shape survives only where the composer, publisher, and copilot still speak it.
